# CCAO-F: Claude Certified Associate - Foundations

The one Claude certification that requires no code, no API knowledge, and no engineering background. CCAO-F validates that a professional can use Claude as a core productivity tool in a business role: specifying work precisely, judging outputs before acting on them, choosing the right capability and model, setting Claude up for repeatable work, and applying data-protection and governance judgment.

## What the exam actually tests

The product surface a heavy business user touches every day:

- **Projects**: workspaces with instructions, knowledge, Skills, and their own memory
- **Artifacts** for deliverables you revise and share
- **Research**, **code-executed analysis** of uploaded files, and **connectors** to Google Drive, Gmail, and calendar
- **Skills** that apply a procedure or template the same way every time
- **Memory and incognito chats**: what Claude keeps, and how you keep it clean
- **Model tiers and effort**: matching cost, speed, and quality to the task

Just as much weight sits on judgment. Output evaluation is the heaviest domain, and governance is close behind: checking outputs against their sources, deciding how much review an output needs, labelling each workflow step as something Claude does, something a person reviews, or something a person keeps, and de-identifying data before it goes anywhere near an analysis. The official guide builds every domain on Anthropic's AI Fluency competencies: Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence.

CCAO-F fits analysts, project managers, operations leads, consultants, and professionals in marketing, communications, HR, and education who use Claude regularly and want a credential that proves it. If you sit between business and engineering and are unsure whether this or the developer exam fits, the deciding question is whether your work happens inside the claude.ai app or in code that calls the API.

## Study path (~3 weeks part-time)

1. **Week 1: official baseline.** Check that your employer is in the [Claude Partner Network](https://claude.com/partners) (registration needs your company email), read the exam guide that matches your exam date, and complete the Partner Academy prep course and the AI Fluency course.
2. **Week 2: deliberate product practice.** Configure a Project with instructions, knowledge, and a Skill for a recurring task; connect a workplace source; verify a research report's citations; analyze a de-identified spreadsheet with code execution; review, edit, and delete memories.
3. **Week 3: calibrate.** Full-length timed practice tests, per-domain review, and a final pass over output evaluation and governance scenarios, which trip up candidates who prepared only on features.

## Common mistakes

- Treating it as a features quiz. Output evaluation and governance together are 37% of the new exam.
- Assuming an incognito chat makes sensitive data safe to share. It controls what is kept, not what you disclosed.
- Fixing a quality drop by rewriting instructions or upgrading the model, when stale knowledge or old memories changed.
- Preparing on the developer platform. The exam covers claude.ai the product; API knowledge earns nothing here.
- Letting the credential lapse: renewal is free before the 12-month mark and a full-price proctored exam after it.
